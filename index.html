A Light on Shakespeare Road

For Pat Fish

I first met Pat at a photo shoot organized by Northampton's short lived but much loved Music For Squares magazine. The idea was local musicians would dress up as their favorite rock/pop star for the day - Freddy Mercury, Bruce Springsteen, Jimi Hendrix etc. I went as Townes Van Zandt, can of coke (full fat) in one hand and bottle of Jack Daniel's in the other. Go and sit next to Kevin Ayers I'm told.

And so began my friendship with Pat Fish, The Jazz Butcher, last of the gentleman adventures, hard water kid of the asbestos age and the only person in the room that didn't need to pretend to be rock and roll legend.

I don't have a horde of songs from the last 15 years. I often wonder why. I think I probably just stopped looking for them. But of course some songs find you and they are usually the ones that fuck you up.

More people heard Making History for the first time in the warmth of Pat's living room than probably anywhere else. So it seems fitting to dedicate its rerelease to him.

I guess like a lot of people, this list of things I wished I'd asked Pat while he was still on this planet gets longer every day, but the one thing I want to say is - thank you, Pat xx
