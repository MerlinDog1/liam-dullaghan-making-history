A Light on Shakespeare Road

For Pat Fish

I first met Pat at a photo shoot organized by Northampton's short lived but much loved Music For Squares magazine. The idea was local musicians would dress up as a rock/pop star for the day - Freddy Mercury, Bruce Springsteen, Jimi Hendrix. I went as Townes Van Zandt, can of coke (full fat) in one hand and bottle of Jack Daniel's in the other. Go and sit next to Kevin Ayers I'm told. So began my friendship with Pat.

I don't have horde of songs from the last 15 years. I often wonder why. I think I probably just stopped looking for them. But of course then there are the songs that find you, and they are usually the ones that fuck you up. It's included here as a way of dedicating the reissue to Pat.

More people heard Making History for the first time in the warmth of Pat's living room than anywhere else. I guess like a lot of people, this list of things I wished I'd asked Pat while he was still on this planet gets longer every day, but the one thing I want to say is - thank you, Pat xx
